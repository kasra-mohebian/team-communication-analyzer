# References and source provenance

The original implementation and its reference list are preserved in [the upstream record](UPSTREAM.md). This combined index adds navigable source links and distinguishes method references from dataset rights. Citing a lexicon or model does not independently validate the five role categories.

## Conceptual and team background

- Gloor, P. (2022). *Happimetrics*. [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.4337/9781803924021).
- Gloor, P. A. & Claude (2025). *Cybernetic Alchemy*, conceptual seminar source. The chapter/book is not redistributed.
- Kozlowski & Ilgen (2006). *Enhancing the Effectiveness of Work Groups and Teams*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1529-1006.2006.00030.x).
- Edmondson (1999). *Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/2666999). These outcomes were not directly measured by this prototype.

## Data sources

- Chatterjee et al. (2020). *Software-related Slack Chats with Disentangled Conversations*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3379597.3387493), [repository](https://github.com/preethac/Software-related-Slack-Chats-with-Disentangled-Conversations), [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/3627124). No raw corpus is bundled.
- Nankani (2020). *WhatsApp chat data analysis*. [Repository](https://github.com/tusharnankani/whatsapp-chat-data-analysis), [MIT notice](https://github.com/tusharnankani/whatsapp-chat-data-analysis/blob/main/LICENSE). Dataset privacy and software-licence scope are separate questions.
- Mbada et al. (2023). *Social presence and dynamics of group communication*. [PLOS ONE and supplements](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288773). See DATA.md for the timestamp limitation and parser mismatch with the historical clean copy.
- Garimella & Eckles (2020). Indian WhatsApp corpus, considered and excluded from configured training; its size must not be counted as training volume.
